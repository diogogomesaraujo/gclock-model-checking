COUNTER EXAMPLE (lagging clock catches up):

1. P2 is a genesis party: reference (slot 0, time 0), synced.
2. Two Continues: counter = 2, real slot = 1. P2 diffuses "slot 1".
3. P1 joins late (registers at counter 1). Its localclock is 1, its
   localclockZ is still 0, so Sigma = 1 (Z has not advanced its clock yet).
4. P1 receives P2's block and reads its clock: 0.
   It sets reference (slot 1, time 0).
5. One more Continue: counter = 3, real slot = floor(3/2) = 1.
   Z sends two Increases to P1: localclockZ = 2, Sigma back to 0.
6. P1 outputs S_Map = 1 + floor((2 - 0) / 2) = 2.

P1 outputs slot 2 (one slot ahead), real slot is 1.
Z tells the two worlds apart.
