Pi_roundSync

start_round(round):
    for each peer in P:
        send round_message to peer via F_bd_smt
    send "RoundOK" to F_clock

poll_and_advance():
    while not received_all_messages_for(round):
        for each peer in P:
            msg = fetch_from_channel(peer)
            if msg is not null:
                received[peer][round] = msg
    round = round + 1
    output ("RoundComplete", round)

Distinguisher Strategy
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
1. Send inputs for Round 1 to all honest parties.
2. Instruct network channel F_bd_smt to delay messages by max delay (delta).
3. Observe when Party P_2 outputs Round 2:
   - If P_2 advances at step 1     --> Ideal World (F_clock)
   - If P_2 hangs until step delta --> Real World (Network delay)
